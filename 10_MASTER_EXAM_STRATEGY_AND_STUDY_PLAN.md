## 1. Why Calendar-Based Schedules Fail & How This System Works
Traditional study calendars (e.g. "Day 1 do this, Day 2 do that") fail during medical school exam preparation because unexpected clinical rotations, hospital duties, and varying module complexities derail rigid timelines.

Instead, this system uses a **Concurrent 3-Track Mastery Engine**. You progress through defined **Milestone Modules** at your own speed, ensuring complete coverage across **Theory**, **Objective (MCQs)**, and **Practical/OSCE**.

```
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                THE CONCURRENT 3-TRACK STUDY ENGINE                                 |
+------------------------------------+--------------------------------+------------------------------+
|     TRACK 1: THEORY FOUNDATIONS    |   TRACK 2: ACTIVE MCQ DRILLING | TRACK 3: PRACTICAL & OSCE    |
| (Pathology, Criteria & Mechanisms) | (Active Recall & Question Bank)| (Spotters, Tests & Lab Math) |
+------------------------------------+--------------------------------+------------------------------+
| Master disease frameworks, WHO/FAB | Solve departmental MCQs daily  | Practice blood film & marrow |
| criteria, staging, and algorithms. | to calibrate exact cutoffs and | spotters, lab calculations,  |
| Focus on essay-writing structure.  | distractor traps.              | electrophoresis, & grouping. |
+------------------------------------+--------------------------------+------------------------------+
```

## 2. The 6 Mastery Milestones

### 🚩 Milestone 1: Foundations & Red Cell Anemias (FPPP & GPP)
* **Target Materials:** `01_FOUNDATIONS_AND_HEMOPOIESIS.md` and `02_RED_CELL_DISORDERS_AND_ANEMIAS.md`.
* **Theory Objectives:**
  * Diagram the cellular and ECM components of the Hemopoietic Inductive Microenvironment (HIM).
  * Draw and explain the duodenal iron absorption pathway and Hepcidin regulation.
  * Reproduce from memory the Microcytic Anemia Differential Diagnosis Table (IDA vs ACD vs Thal Minor vs Sideroblastic).
  * Explain the biochemical mechanism of Subacute Combined Degeneration in B12 deficiency.
  * Contrast Intravascular vs Extravascular Hemolysis laboratory parameters.
  * State the Camitta criteria for Severe Aplastic Anemia.
* **Practical / Spotter Check:**
  * Spherocytes vs Schistocytes vs Bite cells.
  * Hypersegmented neutrophils vs Megaloblasts in marrow.
  * Capillary tube PCV calculation and MCH/MCV/MCHC formulas.

### 🚩 Milestone 3: Hematologic Malignancies (SPP 301)
* **Target Materials:** `04_HEMATOLOGIC_MALIGNANCIES.md` and `08_CASE_BASED_STUDIES_SOLUTIONS.md` (Cases 1 & 2).
* **Theory Objectives:**
  * Compare and contrast AML vs ALL (age, morphology, cytochemistry MPO/PAS, immunophenotype, sanctuaries).
  * Detail Acute Promyelocytic Leukemia (APML): $t(15;17)$, faggot cells, DIC risk, ATRA/ATO mechanism.
  * Explain CML: Philadelphia chromosome $t(9;22)$, BCR-ABL1 tyrosine kinase, low LAP score, Imatinib therapy.
  * Detail CLL: Smudge cells, CD5+ CD19+ CD23+, Rai and Binet staging systems, Richter transformation.
  * Contrast Hodgkin Lymphoma (RS cells, CD15+ CD30+, Ann Arbor staging) vs Non-Hodgkin Lymphoma (DLBCL, Follicular $t(14;18)$, Burkitt $t(8;14)$ starry sky).
  * State the CRAB criteria and bone marrow cytology of Multiple Myeloma.
* **Practical / Spotter Check:**
  * Auer rods in AML blasts.
  * Smudge cells in CLL.
  * Reed-Sternberg owl-eye cells in Hodgkin lymphoma.
  * Rouleaux formation and clock-face plasma cells in Multiple Myeloma.

### 🚩 Milestone 4: Hemostasis, Coagulation & Spleen (SPP 302)
* **Target Materials:** `05_HEMOSTASIS_THROMBOSIS_AND_SPLEEN.md`.
* **Theory Objectives:**

  * Trace the extrinsic, intrinsic, and common coagulation pathways and correlate with PT/INR, APTT, and TT.

  * Detail the pathogenesis, pentad ("FAT RN"), and emergency management of TTP vs ITP.
  * Outline the diagnostic profile of DIC (PT, APTT, Platelets, Fibrinogen, D-Dimer).
  * Describe the anatomy of the spleen (Red vs White pulp, Culling vs Pitting), causes of massive splenomegaly, and post-splenectomy OPSI prophylaxis.
* **Practical / Spotter Check:**
  * Teardrop poikilocytes in Primary Myelofibrosis.
  * Coagulation laboratory report interpretation.
