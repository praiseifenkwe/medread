## 🎯 Welcome & Curriculum Architecture
This comprehensive study suite has been systematically engineered from the official 41 curriculum lecture presentations, clinical case-based studies (CBL), departmental timetables, and practice question banks of the 300 Level Hematology & Blood Transfusion course.

It is structured to guarantee mastery across all three pillars of the medical professional examination:
1. **THEORY (Essays & Short Answer Questions - SAQs):** Etiologies, pathophysiological cascades, diagnostic criteria (WHO & FAB), staging systems, differential diagnosis tables, and clinical management algorithms.
2. **OBJECTIVE (Single Best Answer MCQs & Vignettes):** High-yield numerical cutoffs, laboratory normal ranges, storage temperatures, shelf-lives, chromosomal translocations, immunophenotypic markers (CD markers), and drug mechanisms of action.
3. **PRACTICAL & OSCE (Slide Spotters, Lab Tests & Case Workups):** Peripheral blood smear morphology, bone marrow cytology, hemocytometer chamber calculations, PCV/ESR/reticulocyte determinations, hemoglobin electrophoresis band patterns, blood grouping, crossmatching, Coombs testing, and coagulation profiles.

## 🏆 Key Exam Tips from Departmental Professors

### 1. Prof. A.S. Akanmu (Hemopoiesis & Anemias)
* **High-Yield Emphasis:** Expect detailed questions on the **Haemopoietic Inductive Microenvironment (HIM)** components (extracellular matrix vs cellular stroma), cytokine receptor families (JAK-STAT signaling), and **Iron Metabolism regulation by Hepcidin**.
* **Common Trap:** Conflating **Iron Deficiency Anemia (low ferritin, high TIBC)** with **Anemia of Chronic Disease (high ferritin, low TIBC)**. Always look at the bone marrow iron store (Perl's Prussian blue stain) as the definitive differentiator.

### 2. Prof. T.A. Adeyemo (Leukemias & Hemoglobinopathies)
* **High-Yield Emphasis:** Chromosomal translocations and targeted therapy: **t(9;22) BCR-ABL1 in CML** (treated with Imatinib) and **t(15;17) PML-RARA in APML/AML-M3** (treated with ATRA + ATO to prevent fatal DIC).
* **Case Scenarios:** Always be vigilant for clinical presentations featuring acute mucosal bleeding + blasts with Auer rods or "faggot cells". That is APML with secondary DIC!

### 3. Dr. Adewoyin (Haemostasis & Lymphomas)
* **High-Yield Emphasis:** Coagulation laboratory algorithms: **1:1 Mixing Studies**. If APTT corrects upon mixing, it is a factor deficiency (Hemophilia A/B); if it fails to correct, it is an inhibitor (Lupus anticoagulant or Factor VIII autoantibody).
* **Lymphoma Differentiation:** Reed-Sternberg cells in Hodgkin Lymphoma (**CD15+, CD30+, CD20-**) vs NHL (frequently extranodal, B-cell predominant, CD20+).

### 4. Dr. A.A. Ogbenna & Dr. Elebute-Odunsi (Transfusion Medicine & Practical Lab)
* **High-Yield Emphasis:** **Transfusion reactions timeline and differentiation**: TRALI (anti-HLA/HNA antibodies, non-cardiogenic pulmonary edema, normal BNP) vs TACO (fluid overload, cardiogenic pulmonary edema, high BNP, responds to furosemide).
* **Storage Parameters:** Platelets at 20-24°C with continuous agitation (5 days); PRBCs at 2-6°C (35-42 days); FFP at -18°C or colder (1 year).
