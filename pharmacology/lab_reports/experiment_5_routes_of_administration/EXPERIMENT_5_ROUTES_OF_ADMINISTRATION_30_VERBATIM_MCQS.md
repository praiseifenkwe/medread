## 🎯 High-Yield Conceptual Summary for In-Course Exams

1. **Why Route Matters (Pharmacokinetic Determinants of Onset & Duration):**
   * **Intraperitoneal (IP):** Very rapid onset due to massive surface area and rich vascular/mesenteric capillary networks of the peritoneal cavity. *(Note: Drugs absorbed IP enter portal circulation and undergo first-pass hepatic metabolism).*
   * **Intramuscular (IM):** Rapid onset; rate depends on vascular perfusion of the specific muscle group (deltoid > vastus lateralis > gluteus maximus).
   * **Subcutaneous (SC):** Slower, sustained onset due to lower vascularity and higher adipose tissue resistance compared to muscle.
   * **Oral (PO):** Slowest onset; delayed by gastric emptying time, dilution by gastric contents, intestinal mucosal barrier transit, and obligatory hepatic first-pass metabolism.
2. **Standardized Clinical Endpoint: Loss of Righting Reflex (LORR):**
   * The **righting reflex** is an involuntary motor reflex mediated by the vestibular apparatus, cerebellum, and brainstem reticular formation that restores a quadruped to its feet when placed on its dorsum.
   * **Loss of Righting Reflex (LORR)** is the gold-standard preclinical surrogate marker for the onset of surgical anesthesia and loss of consciousness in laboratory rodents.
3. **Pentobarbitone (Nembutal) Pharmacology:**
   * Short-acting oxybarbiturate.
   * **Mechanism of Action:** Binds to allosteric sites on the neuronal $\text{GABA}_\text{A}$ receptor complex $\rightarrow$ prolongs the open duration of chloride ($Cl^-$) ion channels $\rightarrow$ causes membrane hyperpolarization $\rightarrow$ depresses CNS activity. At high concentrations, it directly opens chloride channels and depresses excitatory AMPA receptors.
4. **Methodological Controls:**
   * **Use of Male Rats:** Eliminates cyclical variations in hepatic cytochrome P450 enzyme activity and plasma binding proteins driven by the 4–5 day rodent estrous cycle (estrogen and progesterone).
   * **Weight Standardization:** Dose is strictly fixed at **100 mg/kg** across all routes to isolate the *route of administration* as the sole independent variable.
