# EXPERIMENT 3: INDIVIDUAL VARIATIONS IN THE RESPONSE TO DRUGS

1. The major purpose of this experiment is to demonstrate:

A. Drug incompatibility
B. Individual variation in drug response
C. Drug excretion
D. Drug absorption

Answer: B

A. Diazepam
